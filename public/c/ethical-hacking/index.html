Ethical Hacking & Cybersecurity

Master the art of defensive and offensive security. Learn to responsibly identify, exploit, and report security vulnerabilities using industry-leading hacking tools.

About the Course

Our Ethical Hacking and Cybersecurity course (Junior Penetration Tester program) is designed to turn tech enthusiasts into professional security analysts and ethical hackers. Since 2012, Let Solutions has provided hands-on, practical-oriented technical education in Kerala.

This course takes you from scratch to intermediate and advanced pentesting domains. You will get deep exposure to computer networking security, vulnerability scans, system exploits, web security, and credential recovery. We emphasize complete hands-on labs with zero dry theory, simulating actual cyberattacks in controlled environments so you develop true troubleshooting and exploitation expertise.

Upon completion, you will be well-equipped with the state-of-the-art tools and concepts demanded by security operations centers and penetration testing teams globally, backed by Let Solutions' legendary technical support.
