Chip-Level Electronics

Laptop Chip Level Repairing Course

Master advanced electronics repair. Learn to diagnose and fix motherboard issues, BGA chips, power circuits, and bios firmware at component level.

About the Course

Our Master Diploma in Chip Level Engineering is standard-setting vocational training designed for individuals aiming to become professional hardware and motherboard electronics repair specialists.

This training program is 100% lab-based. You will gain extensive hand-on practice under the guidance of expert instructors in diagnostics, fault-tracing, power rail analysis, BIOS flashing, and chip replacement using advanced soldering and rework stations.

Whether you want to establish your own service center or secure a high-paying diagnostic engineer role, this course equips you with complete diagnostic logic, tools knowledge, and lifetime backup support to guarantee your career launch.

What You Will Learn

01

Electronics Fundamentals & SMD

Study components, resistance, capacitance, diode characteristics, and master high-precision SMD soldering techniques.

02

Schematics, Boardview & Tracing

Learn motherboard block diagram tracing, circuit schematics, boardview analysis, and diagnostic logic signals (S5, S3, S0 states).

03

Power Section & IC Diagnostics

Identify and troubleshoot power rails, PWM IC sections, buck-boost controllers, VRM modules, and charging circuit faults.

04

BGA Rework, Reballing & Flashing

Master BGA station operations, chip reballing, BIOS/UEFI programming using RT809F/RT809H, and Super I/O chip configurations.
